Capsule

A room-scale latex membrane, cast from architectural surfaces and wired with contact microphones, that turns a space into a self-oscillating instrument.

Latex / contact microphones / transducers / Serge Modular / Zoom L6 / found scrap wood — with Alex Wu. Every component is input and output at once. Visitors touch the walls and hear their own presence returned as sound.

Tap Room

Four cameras watch a room and answer only in sound. Movement through the space drives a seven-voice ensemble — surveillance logic inverted into an invitation to move.

Max/MSP/Jitter / four webcams / seven voices / modular drone — RISD Memorial Hall. Deterministic mapping that never performs the same way twice. No screen, no interface, no image of what it sees.

Two Instruments

A reverb carrying the memory of a sealed railroad tunnel, and an oscillator whose waveform is governed by physics. Both replace something stored with something that behaves.

Max/MSP gen~ / Electrosmith Daisy Patch SM / hand-wired panels — Serge format and Eurorack. A four-channel feedback delay network, and a 64-node circular string that reports its own kinetic energy as control voltage.
